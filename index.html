        Failure patterns

        deterministic clustering over the fixture run logs

        Failure messages normalize into signatures: numbers, hashes and casing collapse, so one recurring error stays one cluster. Retry recoveries separate flaky clusters from hard ones, and failed attempts rank by the billable minutes they burned.
