This is PipelinePulse, a CI pipeline health and cost studio. Paste a GitHub Actions style workflow or load a fixture, and a hand written YAML parser builds the whole model right in your browser, refusing bad input with line numbers instead of guessing. The pipeline map lays out every job in topological waves with hairline dependency edges, and traces the critical path in green. Duration math computes medians and p ninety from run history, then simulates runner queues so you can see waiting time emerge when a wave outdemands the pool. The cost model bills whole minutes per job and matrix combination from an editable rate table, projects monthly spend with a p ninety bound, and accounts for cache hits. Failure patterns normalize numbers and hashes so one recurring error stays one cluster across the whole matrix, separates flaky from hard failures, and ranks jobs by burned billable minutes. The savings bench prices five concrete optimizations, each labeled estimate with its assumptions, and the total is honestly marked an upper bound.
